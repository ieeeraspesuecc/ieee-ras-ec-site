FLAGSHIP HACKATHON

RoboCraft — 2-Day Robotics Hackathon

Dates: TBA  ·  Venue: TBA

Code, build, and innovate! Teams of 2–4 design and construct a working robotic creation from scratch, live over two days — AI, automation, IoT, or pure robotics magic, your theme, your call. Bring a functional prototype, sensors and actuators driven by real software, to the judging floor.

Full Details — Rules, Prizes & Project Ideas

What You'll Gain

  • Hands-on build time with Arduino, Raspberry Pi, sensors & actuators
  • Mentorship from experienced mentors and faculty
  • Networking with fellow robotics enthusiasts and industry experts
  • A portfolio-ready project for your resume and GitHub
  • IEEE RAS participation certificate for every team member

Rules

  • Teams of 2–4, open robotics theme
  • Built entirely during the event — no pre-built prototypes
  • Original code only (credited open-source libraries OK)
  • At least 1 member must attend the full event + presentation
  • Mentors can guide, but can't code or build for you
  • Submit: working prototype, live demo, source code, 5-min presentation, README

Project Inspiration

  • Autonomous obstacle-avoiding robot
  • Gesture-controlled robotic arm
  • Line-following robot with IoT
  • Self-balancing robot
  • Smart home automation robot

Prizes & Recognition

  • 🥇 1st Place — Cash prize + Trophy + IEEE Feature
  • 🥈 2nd Place — Cash prize + Certificate
  • 🥉 3rd Place — Cash prize + Certificate
  • 🌟 Best Innovation & Best Beginner Team awards
  • 🎖️ IEEE RAS certificate for all participants
